Best Free Synthesia Alternatives, Tested (2026)

By Jones Dwell · Last verified 2026-10-17

Synthesia's free Basic plan is real — 10 minutes a month — but exports carry a watermark and the generate button demands an account. If that's not your deal, here are three tested alternatives with genuine free tiers, each with its watermark policy and signup wall labeled.

VisionStory: the 720p free tier

VisionStory's free plan hands out 10 credits — roughly a minute of talking-head video — plus 4 refill credits every week. Exports are 720p and watermarked, capped at 30 seconds per video. The credits only go to registered users, so the signup wall sits before the free tier, not after it. Best when you want short avatar clips and don't mind the weekly drip.

Puppetry: preview before you sign up

Puppetry is the only one of the three whose studio you can browse anonymously — you can look around before committing to an account. The free tier allows 3 creations a month, all watermarked. It's the smallest free allowance here, but the try-before-you-sign-up flow is the most honest of the bunch.

HeyGen: the upgrade path

HeyGen's free plan gives 3 videos a month, up to a minute each, with what it calls a "discreet watermark." Don't be fooled by its "Without Watermark" page title — we checked, and the page itself admits free exports carry watermarks. Where HeyGen wins: the paid upgrade is the cleanest of the three, with watermark removal from $29/month. Pick it if you expect to outgrow the free tier.

FAQ

Is there a free alternative to Synthesia?

Yes — three tested: VisionStory (10 free credits, 720p), Puppetry (3 free creations/month), and HeyGen (3 free videos/month). All have genuine free tiers, and all watermark their free exports.

Which free Synthesia alternative gives the most video?

Synthesia's own Basic plan is the most generous at 10 minutes/month. Among the alternatives, HeyGen gives 3 videos up to 1 minute each per month; VisionStory gives about 1 minute plus weekly refills; Puppetry gives 3 creations per month.

Do free Synthesia alternatives add watermarks?

Yes. All three alternatives we tested — VisionStory, Puppetry, and HeyGen — watermark their free exports, as does Synthesia's own free tier. The only verified watermark-free free video tool is Pixlr, which is a general video generator rather than an avatar tool.

Do I need a credit card for these free tiers?

No. VisionStory, Puppetry, HeyGen, and Synthesia's Basic plan are all card-free at the free level.
