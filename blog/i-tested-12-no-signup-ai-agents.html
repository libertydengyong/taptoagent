Free AI Chatbot No Sign Up: I Tested 12, 7 Actually Work

Published 2026-10-11 · 6 min read

"No sign-up required" is the most lied-about phrase on the internet. Half the tools that claim it hit you with a login wall the moment you try anything useful. So I opened 12 of them and actually used each one — no account, no email, no tricks.

How I tested

  • Front door test: can I get a real answer/output without creating any account?
  • Usefulness test: is the free tier actually usable, or a 2-message demo begging for signup?
  • Honesty test: does it stay account-free, or does the wall appear mid-session?

The 7 that passed

Duck.ai — the daily driver

DuckDuckGo's chat. Open it, pick a model (GPT, Claude, Llama, Mistral), start typing. No account exists to create. Anonymous by design, not by accident. This is the one I actually kept using after testing.

LMArena — the most fun way to compare models

Two anonymous models answer side by side, you vote for the better one. Completely account-free, and weirdly addictive. The honest way to find out which model is best right now.

Venice.ai — private by default

Stateless chat — nothing saved unless you opt in. Core use needs no signup. Slightly rougher edges than Duck.ai, but the privacy posture is the real deal.

Pollinations.ai — the API that happens to have a website

Free text and image generation, no key, no account. I generated a dozen images without a single prompt to register. Developers: the API is the real product here.

Perchance — unlimited and unpretentious

One of the last truly unlimited account-free platforms. Ad-supported, zero barriers. Not the smartest models, but for casual chat and image play, the price (free, no account) is unbeatable.

Craiyon — dumb, fun, free

Nine images per prompt, no account, no limits. Won't win quality awards, but for memes and quick concepts it's instant.

Raphael AI — the surprise

Clean image generation with no account wall. Less known than the others, which is exactly why it's on this list — the famous ones all want your email eventually.

The 5 that failed (or half-failed)

I'm not naming every disappointment, but the patterns are worth knowing:

  • The bait-and-switch: works for 3 messages, then "create a free account to continue." That's not no-signup, that's a trial.
  • The crippleware: technically no account, but the free tier is so limited it's a demo, not a tool.
  • The wall moves: no signup today, signup wall next month. I re-check listings regularly — see the directory.

The takeaway

Genuinely account-free AI still exists, but it's shrinking. The 7 above all passed the front-door test today — bookmark this page, because the list changes. For the full maintained directory with one-line reviews of each, see the no-signup agents directory.
