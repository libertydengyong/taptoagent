Magic Hour: the promise dies at the button

This is why we test instead of reading headlines. Magic Hour AI's search result literally reads "Free AI Video Generator Online (No Sign Up)." We opened the text-to-video editor anonymously — no login, and it worked. We picked a template, typed a prompt, and clicked "Generate 4s Video." Instantly: a signup dialog. "Create a free account to generate." The no-signup promise covers browsing the editor, not generating a single frame. Behind the wall sits a real free allowance — 400 one-time credits, roughly four to five short clips, no credit card — and free exports carry watermarks. It's a legitimate free tier wrapped in a misleading headline.

invideo AI: there is no free tier to test

invideo AI ranks for "free AI video generator" with a 4.7-star listing, so we checked. Clicking "Start Creating" redirected us straight to a signup page before any generation was possible. Then the pricing page settled it: three paid plans only — Starter at $20, Plus at $60, Max at $150 per seat per month. The word "free" on invideo's site refers to guest seats bundled with paid plans. There is no free tier, no trial mentioned, nothing to test anonymously. Ranking for "free" without offering one is the oldest trick in this niche, and it still works.

Runway: real credits, real wall

Runway is the honest one of the three — free credits are real (125 one-time credits, about two 5-second Gen-4.5 videos), no credit card required, and the signup wall appears exactly where you'd expect it: the moment you try to generate. Guest mode even lets you browse the interface first. One blemish worth noting: Runway's own pages disagree with each other. The pricing page says 125 one-time credits; the signup popup says 500. Both are quoted verbatim from their site on the same day. When a company can't agree with itself about its free tier, treat the smaller number as the truth.

Why video generation resists the no-signup shelf

Compare this with our talking-head test: three real free tiers, all card-free, all watermarked. Video generation is the same story with higher stakes — GPU time costs real money, so every free tier is metered, watermarked, and gated behind an account. The no-signup shelf that keeps growing for chatbots and research agents simply doesn't extend to video generation. If a video tool promises no signup, expect the wall at the generate button — we clicked it so you don't have to.

FAQ

Is there a free AI video generator with no sign up?

Not among the three we tested. Magic Hour AI advertises "No Sign Up" but demands an account the moment you click generate. invideo AI has no free tier at all. Runway ML offers free credits but only after signup.

Does Magic Hour AI really work without signing up?

No. The editor opens anonymously and you can enter a prompt, but clicking the generate button pops up a "Create a free account to generate" wall. The no-signup claim covers browsing, not generating.

Does invideo AI have a free plan?

No. invideo AI's pricing page lists only three paid plans (Starter $20, Plus $60, Max $150 per seat/month). The word "free" on their site refers to guest seats bundled with paid plans, not a free tier.

How much can I generate for free on Runway?

Runway's free tier gives 125 one-time credits — roughly two 5-second Gen-4.5 videos. Signup is required before generating, no credit card needed. Note: Runway's own signup popup mentions 500 credits, contradicting its pricing page.

Do free AI video generators add watermarks?

Magic Hour's watermark-free exports are limited to paid plans, so free videos carry watermarks. Runway lists "no watermarks" only on Standard plans and above, implying free output is watermarked.
