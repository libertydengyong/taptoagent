Watermark policies are quoted from official pages, not inferred. We re-check because free quotas change without announcement.

FAQ

Is there a truly free AI video generator with no signup?

No. We tested 11 video generators and every single one requires an account before generating. Video generation costs real GPU money, so the no-signup shelf that exists for chatbots stops here.

Which free AI video generator gives the most?

For talking-head videos: Synthesia's Basic plan (10 minutes/month). For general video: Pixlr's free account (watermark-free, but trial credits are finite). For one-off clips: Runway's 125 one-time credits.

Which free AI video generator has no watermark?

Pixlr is the only one we verified: its official page guarantees watermark-free exports on a free account (signup required, trial credits limited). Every other free tier we tested watermarks its output.

Do I need a credit card for any of these free tiers?

No. None of the verified free tiers require a credit card — Synthesia, Runway, Magic Hour, HeyGen, Pixlr, VisionStory, and Puppetry are all card-free at the free level.
