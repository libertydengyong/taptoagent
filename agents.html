How we verify "no sign up"

Duck.ai

One-line review
DuckDuckGo's private AI chat lets you switch between GPT, Claude, Llama and Mistral models in one interface, with prompts anonymized before they reach providers. There's no account to create and no email to hand over — you just open the page and start chatting. It's the closest thing to a private multi-model chatbot that asks for nothing in return.
Best for
Private everyday chat.
Limits
Daily usage caps on the free tier; chat history stays on your device only.

LMArena

One-line review
LMArena puts two anonymous frontier models side by side: you prompt both, vote for the better answer, then learn which models they were. No login is needed to run battles, making it the cheapest honest way to compare GPT vs Claude vs Gemini on your own questions. An optional account only adds chat history — the core experience is fully anonymous.
Best for
Honestly comparing GPT vs Claude vs Gemini for free.
Limits
Battle format by design; account optional for history.

Venice.ai

One-line review
Venice.ai is a privacy-first AI chat that runs stateless by default — conversations aren't saved unless you explicitly choose to keep them. The core chat works with no signup and no account, which is rare for a tool running capable models. Expect around 10 free text prompts a day on the anonymous tier; it's built for people who want capable AI without a paper trail.
Best for
Private long-form conversations.
Limits
Free tier around 10 text prompts/day; image prompts are filtered on the free plan.

FAQ

Are these AI agents really free with no sign up?

Yes. Each listing was opened in a fresh browser with no account and used for a real task — a chat conversation or an image generation — before it earned a place here. No email, no Google login, no credit card "free trial."

Do I need to install anything?

No. Every agent on this page runs in the browser, with one exception: Brave Leo is built into the Brave browser, so you need Brave installed to use it. Everything else is just a URL.

Is my data private on no-signup AI tools?

"No sign up" describes the front door, not what happens inside. An account-free site can still log IPs or train on prompts. Duck.ai anonymizes prompts before they reach providers, and Venice.ai runs stateless by default — but as a rule, don't paste secrets into any free AI tool, signed up or not.

Why do some free AI tools add a signup wall later?

Free tiers cost real GPU money, and providers tighten access as they grow. When that happens to a listed tool, we re-test it — if the core feature now requires an account, the listing is removed. That's why the "last verified" date matters.

Which no-signup AI chatbot is the best?

For private everyday chat: Duck.ai. For comparing frontier models free: LMArena. For unlimited casual use: Perchance. There's no single winner — read the Duck.ai review, Perchance review, and Pollinations.ai review and pick by your use case.
