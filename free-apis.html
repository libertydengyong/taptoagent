Free AI APIs for Developers

Real, working free tiers from serious providers — not trials that expire in a week. Grab a key (or no key at all) and start building.

✓ Free tier

Google AI Studio

One-line review
The most generous official free API tier in the industry. Sign in with a Google account, get a Gemini key, and build — generous enough for prototypes, learning projects, and low-traffic production use. The catch is rate limits that tighten as you scale, at which point you're expected to enable billing.
Free quota
Generous free tier with per-minute limits — check AI Studio docs for current numbers.
Best for
Production prototypes and learning the Gemini API.

✓ Free tier

NVIDIA NIM

One-line review
Free hosted endpoints for top open models with an OpenAI-compatible API, so your existing code just works by swapping the base URL. NVIDIA keeps a rotating cast of strong open models on free endpoints — ideal when you want serious model quality without renting a GPU.
Free quota
Free nvapi- key; roughly 40 requests/min per model. Base URL: https://integrate.api.nvidia.com/v1
Verified models
moonshotai/kimi-k3 · z-ai/glm-5.3 · deepseek-ai/deepseek-v4.1-flash · google/gemma-4-31b-it
Best for
Serious open-model workloads without a GPU.

✓ Free tier

Groq

One-line review
Blazing-fast inference on custom LPU hardware — the speed is genuinely startling the first time you try it. Tokens stream back so fast it changes what real-time features are feasible. The free tier is rate-limited but needs no credit card to start.
Free quota
Free tier with rate limits; no card to start.
Best for
Latency-sensitive apps and real-time features.

✓ No key needed

Pollinations.ai

One-line review
Text and image APIs with no key at all — the lowest-friction way to call an LLM from code. Just hit the endpoint; there's nothing to sign up for. It's a shared public service running on donations and goodwill, so treat it as infrastructure for demos and experiments, not production.
Free quota
Free under fair use; it's a shared public service.
Best for
Demos, bots, hackathons and experiments.

Open pollinations.ai

✓ Free tier

OpenRouter

One-line review
One key, hundreds of models behind an OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Anything tagged :free costs nothing — perfect for A/B testing models against each other before committing. Free models carry rate limits, and availability rotates, but as a model-discovery tool it's unmatched.
Free quota
Free models carry rate limits; paid models need credits.
Best for
Comparing models behind a single API.

Open openrouter.ai

✓ No account

AI Horde

One-line review
A volunteer GPU network for text and image generation — real people's hardware, shared freely. Use the public anonymous key and there's no account, ever. The tradeoff is the queue: anonymous requests wait behind registered workers, so it's for patient hobbyists, not deadlines.
Free quota
Queue-based; the anonymous key gets lower priority than registered workers.
Best for
Hobbyists and open-source tinkerers.

Open aihorde.net

Quick picker

  • Hard tasks (agents, coding, complex reasoning): NVIDIA's moonshotai/kimi-k3 or z-ai/glm-5.3.
  • Light Q&A / code explanations: google/gemma-4-31b-it on NVIDIA, or Gemini via AI Studio.
  • Need speed: Groq.
  • Zero setup: Pollinations.ai — no key, no signup.

FAQ

Do free AI APIs require a credit card?

None of the six listed here require a credit card to start. Google AI Studio, NVIDIA NIM, Groq and OpenRouter need an account (but no card) for a key; Pollinations.ai and AI Horde need nothing at all.

Which free API is best for beginners?

Google AI Studio — the most generous official free tier and the best docs. For zero-setup experiments, Pollinations.ai needs no key whatsoever.

Can I use these free APIs in production?

For low-traffic projects, yes — but read the rate limits first. Free tiers are designed for prototyping and learning; serious traffic needs a paid plan. Pollinations.ai and AI Horde are community services, not SLAs.

Are the free models any good?

Better than you'd expect. NVIDIA's free endpoints serve genuinely strong open models, Groq serves fast versions of popular ones, and OpenRouter's :free tag lets you test-drive dozens. You won't get frontier flagship quality free, but you get surprisingly close.
